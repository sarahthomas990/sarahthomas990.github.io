About Me

My path to a career in data was a winding one. I started in software engineering in the defense sector, spent years pursuing a PhD in Systematic Theology at Boston College (ABD), was the sole BI analyst for a Texas retail chain, and earned my Master's in Analytics from Georgia Tech before landing at USAA as a Decision Science Analyst. Different fields, same question underneath: how do you reason carefully about something that matters, and explain what you found so someone else can trust it?

I'm drawn to projects that let me work the whole analytical process — clarifying the real question at the heart of a problem, digging into context, figuring out what data and methodologies best fit it, and finally finding the right visualization (or words) to communicate the answer, or, just as often, the new questions it raises.

Outside of work, I enjoy time with my husband and our two opinionated cats, and I always have a few books going: Project Hail Mary, Everyday Zen, and (yes, even off the clock) Brent Dykes' Effective Data Storytelling. My husband and I are committed science fiction and horror fans, and, not to mention, proud Trekkies.

NLP Cluster Labeler

Unsupervised topic discovery over ~13,000 real banking customer service messages (BANKING77). Compares TF-IDF, sentence embeddings, and BERTopic against true intent labels held out for validation, then using the Claude API to turn each discovered topic into a human-readable label. BERTopic's topic assignment scored best (NMI 0.82 against labels it never saw) and is what's explored in an interactive app, currently in progress.
